# ScaffoldX AU Intelligence Brief — 1 July 2026

## 1. Culture & General

- **New financial year lands with a bang.** From today: minimum wage up 4.75% to $26.44/hr, Paid Parental Leave extended to 26 weeks, and Payday Super begins (more below). Good small-talk opener — every AU business owner has this on their mind this morning.
- **State of Origin decider looms.** The series is locked 1-1 after a record 91,671-crowd Game 2 at the MCG; Game 3 goes to Suncorp Stadium, Brisbane. Safe, high-value rapport topic with almost any Australian client this week.
- **PM's banking data breach charges.** Two men have faced court over allegedly accessing PM Albanese's restricted personal banking data, with one linked to accounting firm EY — a live reminder of data-access controls even inside "trusted" professional services firms.

## 2. Business & Market Signals

- **Payday Super goes live today.** Employers must now remit super within 7 business days of each payday (not quarterly), the Small Business Super Clearing House has closed, and new STP fields for super liability/QE reporting are mandatory. This is a forced payroll-systems overhaul for every AU employer, effective immediately.
- **Compliance bundle hits simultaneously.** ASIC fees rise, SMS Sender ID Register registration becomes mandatory for branded texts, new seafood country-of-origin labelling applies to hospitality, and ACCC begins active supermarket price monitoring (Woolworths/Coles). Multiple reporting and process changes landing on the same day.
- **Microsoft deepens AU commitment.** Following its A$25bn AI/cloud investment pledge, Microsoft signed a digital-resilience MOU with the Australian Government (11 June) covering secure cloud, cybersecurity and AI — reinforcing Microsoft-stack momentum (Azure, Copilot) that ScaffoldX's Microsoft automation practice sits directly on.
- **Wesfarmers goes agentic.** Wesfarmers signed a multi-year deal with Google Cloud to deploy agentic AI across Kmart, Officeworks, Priceline and OnePass — a signal that large AU retail/multi-brand groups are moving past pilots into production agentic workflows.
- **AI adoption outpacing governance.** National AI Centre data shows only 21% of AU organisations are scaling AI across functions (42% still experimenting, just 8% fully embedded), with privacy/security concerns and lack of trust cited as the top blockers — a clear gap between enthusiasm and operational maturity.
