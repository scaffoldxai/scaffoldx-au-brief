- **Agentic AI adoption is outpacing governance**: 64% of AU/NZ organisations use agentic AI tools that act without direct human instruction, and 50% admit that use is unapproved or ungoverned — a live risk story boards are starting to ask about.
- **Canberra's five-year Microsoft deal** (effective 1 July) gives the APS enterprise-wide access to Copilot, M365, Azure and Dynamics 365, alongside a new whole-of-government cloud-first mandate — expect flow-on demand from agencies and their contractors for Power Platform and Dynamics implementation support.
