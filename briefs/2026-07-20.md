## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **AUSTRAC Tranche 2 goes live**: AML/CTF obligations now cover lawyers, accountants, real estate agents, conveyancers and trust/company service providers from 1 July 2026, with AUSTRAC enrolment closing 29 July. Thousands of professional services firms now need KYC, screening and suspicious-matter reporting processes overnight.
- **Steadfast's $7.7bn carve-up**: KKR has joined the Amwins/Dragoneer consortium taking Australia's largest insurance broker private — Amwins absorbs the underwriting agency arm, Dragoneer/KKR take retail broking. Two standalone businesses will need to stand up separate reporting and systems stacks.
- **Gold sector consolidation**: Genesis Minerals' bid for Vault Minerals is set to create Australia's third-largest gold producer (~$8.5bn), against the backdrop of Elliott Management's raid on Northern Star. Expect a wave of post-merger finance/ops integration work across the sector.
- **Microsoft's VSA6 whole-of-government deal**: a new five-year Commonwealth agreement (effective 1 July) gives APS agencies stable pricing on Copilot, M365, Azure and Dynamics 365 — a strong signal of accelerating federal AI/cloud rollout, with flow-on demand for implementation partners.
- **Ops hiring stays hot**: 600+ business analyst and 400+ PMO analyst roles open across Australia this month, including a Sydney financial-services role building a PMO function from scratch for M&A integration — a classic pre-automation-engagement signal.
