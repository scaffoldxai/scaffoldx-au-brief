## 2. BUSINESS & MARKET SIGNALS

- **AML/CTF Tranche 2 takes effect 1 July — T-minus two days.** The most significant overhaul of Australia's anti-money-laundering regime since 2006 kicks in Wednesday. Real estate professionals, lawyers, conveyancers, accountants, and trust and company service providers become AUSTRAC reporting entities for the first time. Five substantial new obligations go live simultaneously: documented risk assessments, AML/CTF policies, and mandatory governing-body compliance reporting. Most affected firms have never built these processes from scratch.

- **Enterprise AI moves from pilot to production.** 68% of Australian enterprises have shifted AI initiatives from trial to production, per the Australian Enterprise AI Adoption Report 2026. The defining shift: AI as an operator (agentic, workflow-embedded) is replacing AI as an interface (chatbots). Enterprises integrating AI directly into existing platforms are reporting the fastest productivity gains; those keeping it isolated are not seeing results.

- **Construction digital spend accelerates.** Autodesk's 2026 State of Digital Adoption report finds Australian construction firms now run an average of 6.9 technology tools per business, up from 5.0 in 2024, with 25 cents of every dollar invested going to technology. Revizto's 2026 AEC report adds that 64.4% of Australian respondents are highly concerned about vendor data ownership — more than double the 37.8% global average — signalling appetite for vendor-neutral, client-controlled platforms.

- **Funding up 42%, M&A wave building.** Australian startups raised $1.77B across 82 rounds in the first half of 2026 — a 42% year-on-year increase. PwC's M&A Outlook finds 52% of Australian CEOs are planning major acquisitions in the next three years. Post-merger integration is becoming a standing demand signal.

- **BA and BI hiring remains hot.** Over 1,700 Business Analyst roles are currently listed nationally on Glassdoor, with Power BI developer demand strong across SEEK and Jobstreet. Organisations recruiting at this volume for analytical and operations roles are typically mid-transformation — process gaps are visible before the tooling lands.
