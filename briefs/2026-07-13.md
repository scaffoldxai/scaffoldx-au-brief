# ScaffoldX Australia Intelligence Brief — 13 July 2026

## 1. Culture & General
- **Modi's state visit**: PM Modi wrapped a high-profile Australia leg, signing a uranium export deal and launching an India-Australia Critical Minerals Corridor plus a Defence Innovation Corridor. Warm optics, harder trade politics underneath — good small-talk with clients in mining, energy or defence supply chains.
- **Hospitality squeeze**: The $100bn hospitality sector is leading the country in insolvencies — one in ten food and beverage businesses closed in the year to March, hit by ~18% cost inflation and 60-day payment delays. A sharp reminder that thin-margin sectors are desperate for cost/efficiency wins.
- **Super Netball wrapped its grand final** in early July — solid conversational opener with clients around the country as finals season chatter continues into AFL and NRL.

## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **ANZ deploys Salesforce Agentforce at scale** — first in Asia-Pacific — following a national CRM consolidation that unifies fragmented customer data to cut administrative toil. Signals big banks are done experimenting and moving to production AI agents on top of core CRM.
- **Wesfarmers signs multi-year Google Cloud deal** to roll out agentic AI across Kmart, Officeworks, Priceline and OnePass — a major retail conglomerate standardising automation across brand silos.
- **Federal Government inks 5-year Microsoft deal** (from 1 July) giving the APS enterprise access to Copilot, M365, Azure and Dynamics 365 — a strong tailwind for the Microsoft automation stack across the public and quasi-government sector.
- **APRA/ASIC move to simplify the Financial Accountability Regime** — raising materiality thresholds and halving required updates to accountability maps, with consultation aimed at implementation by end-2026. Every regulated entity (banks, insurers, super funds) will need to rework reporting workflows and accountability-map processes to match.
- **AI governance lagging adoption**: HR and risk leaders warn boards are underestimating AI's workforce and decision-making impact, with governance frameworks not keeping pace with rushed automation rollouts — a live pain point as more mid-market firms adopt agentic tools without controls.

## 3. Opportunity Signals
- **APRA/ASIC FAR reform** — regulated financial services entities must rework accountability maps and reporting by end-2026. *Angle*: proactively approach mid-tier banks, insurers and super funds to redesign reporting workflows and Power BI dashboards ahead of the deadline, positioning as "get compliant and get better data, in one project." **Fit: High**
- **ANZ's Agentforce rollout** — sets a visible benchmark for CRM-plus-AI-agent consolidation. *Angle*: target regional banks, credit unions and insurers who can't afford ANZ's Salesforce spend, offering a Power Platform/Dynamics 365 equivalent — "the ANZ playbook, mid-market budget." **Fit: High**
- **AI governance gap** flagged by HR/risk leaders — boards adopting AI faster than they can govern it. *Angle*: package a lightweight AI governance + audit-trail layer around agentic workflow builds (MCP/agents with logging, approvals, human-in-the-loop) for clients already mid-rollout. **Fit: Medium**
- **Hospitality sector distress** ($100bn sector, record insolvencies, 60-day payment delays). *Angle*: pitch lightweight Smartsheet/Power Automate cashflow and supplier-payment tracking for multi-site hospitality groups needing visibility without an ERP-scale spend. **Fit: Medium**
