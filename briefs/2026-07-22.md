## 2. Business & Market Signals

- **AML/CTF Tranche 2 reforms are now live.** From 1 July, an estimated 80,000–90,000 newly regulated entities — accountants, lawyers, real estate agents — must stand up AML/CTF programs and AUSTRAC's new TTR/SMR reporting forms, with compliance periods now aligned to the Australian financial year.
- **Climate/sustainability reporting has teeth.** "Group 2" entities (meeting two of four size thresholds) must now produce audited sustainability reports alongside financial statements, with Scope 3 emissions data due next year — a fresh, forced reporting build for finance and ESG teams.
- **Anthropic is opening a Sydney office**, hiring locally and deepening ties with Australian institutions — another signal of accelerating enterprise AI investment and a validation point for AI-adoption conversations with clients.
- **ASX and LSEG are modernising the ASX 24 derivatives trading platform** through 2026 — a large, multi-year systems migration in financial market infrastructure, with flow-on integration work for connected participants.
- **Construction tech adoption is climbing**, averaging 7.6 technologies per business (up from 6.9), with BIM, digital twins and integrated project controls now described as "operational baseline" rather than innovation — property/construction clients are past the "why automate" conversation and into "how fast."
