# ScaffoldX AU Brief — 15 July 2026

## 1. Culture & General
- **Tax cut lands, cost-of-living bites.** From 1 July the 16% tax bracket dropped to 15% (up to $268 back per taxpayer this year), but CPI is running at 4.2% (April) driven by electricity (+25% y/y), transport and housing — the "tax cut vs. bills" tension is dominating small talk.
- **ASX near records.** Mining and banking strength pushed the market higher this week; Macquarie Group hit a record high and BHP led gains on the back of Rio Tinto's strong iron ore and copper output — good icebreaker with corporate clients.
- **Cricket:** Australia face India in a T20 World Cup showdown at Lord's on Saturday — a live talking point heading into the weekend.

## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **Albanese creates an Office of AI.** In a major speech, the PM announced a "world-first" national AI framework — legal standards for data centres (energy underwriting, net-generation requirements) and mandatory AI standards legislation targeted for early 2027. Boards and mid-market operators should expect governance and reporting obligations to follow. [SBS News](https://www.sbs.com.au/news/podcast-episode/a-generational-opportunity-pm-unveils-new-national-standards-for-artificial-intelligence-in-major-speech/hgmbjjkd3), [Forbes Australia](https://www.forbes.com.au/news/innovation/albanese-creates-office-of-ai-so-australia-can-be-more-than-a-data-warehouse/)
- **Commonwealth's 5-year Microsoft deal starts.** From 1 July, the APS gets whole-of-government access to Copilot, M365, Azure and Dynamics 365 under a new cloud-first mandate — a strong signal for demand in Power Automate/Dynamics/Azure delivery capacity across government and its contractors. [Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366639595/Australia-inks-five-year-deal-with-Microsoft-to-drive-AI-and-cloud-adoption)
- **ANZ scales Agentforce.** ANZ Bank is rolling out AI agents inside a new CRM, becoming the first APAC bank to deploy Salesforce Agentforce at scale to cut administrative toil for business bankers — a bellwether for CRM/automation spend among challenger and regional banks.
- **TPG to acquire Zembl (~$250m EV).** Sydney-founded Zembl heads into TPG's fold, adding another integration workload to the telco/tech M&A pipeline — merged entities routinely need consolidated reporting and systems rationalisation.
- **ASIC/APRA simplify the Financial Accountability Regime.** Announced 16 June, changes raise materiality thresholds and cut accountability-map detail, with consultation aimed at implementation by end 2026 — every FAR entity (banks, insurers, super funds) needs to rework compliance reporting workflows before then. [ASIC](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-121mr-asic-and-apra-announce-far-changes-to-reduce-administrative-burden/)
