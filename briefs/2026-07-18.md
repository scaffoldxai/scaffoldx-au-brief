## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **Office of AI stood up inside PM's department**: Canberra announced a new Office of AI plus mandatory Australian Standards covering data centre energy/water, siting, and creator-consent rules for training data, with legislation flagged for early 2027. Direct implications for any client's AI governance and documentation trail.
- **Payday Super now live**: Since 1 July, employers must remit super within 7 business days of each payday (not quarterly), and the ATO's Small Business Super Clearing House is closing. Payroll and reporting systems across every mid-market client need reconfiguration now.
- **AML/CTF regime expands 1 July**: Real estate agents, precious-metals dealers, lawyers, conveyancers and accountants are newly captured, with a shift to risk-based reporting — a fresh compliance/reporting build for professional services and property clients.
- **Gold sector consolidation**: Genesis Minerals lodged an A$5.6bn proposal to merge with Vault Minerals, creating the ASX's third-largest gold producer (~$12.6bn market cap) with A$2bn in projected synergies; separately, Elliott Management is pressuring Northern Star toward a sale. Expect system and finance-consolidation integration work.
- **AI adoption broadening**: NAB Economics data shows 42% of Australian SMEs now use AI tools (14% more planning to), led by Property Services (69%), Finance & Insurance (64%) and Business Services (61%) — matched by rising board-level concern over governance and oversight gaps.
