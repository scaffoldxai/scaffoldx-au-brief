## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **Payday Super compliance crunch**: employers must now pay and report super every payday (within 7 business days), with Qualifying Earnings and super liability both reported via STP. Xero research shows ~1 in 3 small businesses expect to dip into savings or borrow to comply, and 82% plan to delay investment. ATO grace period runs to 30 June 2027 for "low risk" employers only — a narrow window. ([Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/newsroom/news/payday-super-new-rules-starting-1-july-2026), [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/business-advice/compliance-crunch-facing-small-businesses-july-payday-super/))
- **AML/CTF "Tranche 2" reforms live**: from 1 July, more businesses (real estate, legal, accounting, and others) must enrol with AUSTRAC and run identity verification and record-keeping — a fresh compliance-driven process build for firms that never needed one before.
- **KPMG Australia to cut up to 1,000 jobs, slash partner pay ~20%**: fallout from the audit-leak scandal and a consulting slump (AFR, 14–15 July). Signals continued softness in Big 4 advisory demand and mid-market clients rethinking who delivers PMO/reporting work. ([Going Concern](https://www.goingconcern.com/layoff-watch-26-big-cuts-are-coming-at-kpmg-australia/))
- **APS–Microsoft five-year deal starts 1 July**: federal government locks in Dynamics 365, Power Platform and cloud pricing certainty — a strong tailwind for AI/automation and Power BI delivery capacity across the public sector supply chain. ([Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366639595/Australia-inks-five-year-deal-with-Microsoft-to-drive-AI-and-cloud-adoption))
- **Microsoft 365 price rises up to 33%** landed 1 July across commercial/government/nonprofit tiers — expect cost-conscious clients asking how to get more out of their existing Microsoft stack rather than add seats. ([Evocate](https://www.evocate.com.au/blog/licenses/microsoft-365-price-increase-july-2026-what-australian-organisations-need-to-know/))

## 3. Opportunity Signals
- **Payday Super deadline pressure** — signal: manual/quarterly payroll processes can't meet 7-day STP reporting. Angle: build an automated Power BI/Power Automate dashboard reconciling payroll runs against super liability and STP lodgement, flagging breaches before the ATO does. Fit: **High**.
- **AML/CTF Tranche 2 newly-captured businesses** (real estate, legal, accounting firms) — signal: no existing KYC/onboarding infrastructure. Angle: Power Automate + SharePoint client-onboarding workflow with built-in identity verification and audit trail for AUSTRAC record-keeping. Fit: **High**.
- **KPMG Australia downsizing** — signal: mid-market clients losing continuity on Big 4-run PMO/reporting engagements. Angle: position ScaffoldX as the leaner, faster-mobilising alternative for Smartsheet/PMO and BI reporting work being re-tendered. Fit: **Medium**.
- **APS–Microsoft five-year deal** — signal: agencies committing to Dynamics 365/Power Platform at scale need implementation and reporting partners. Angle: outreach positioning ScaffoldX's Power BI/Power Automate delivery track record for subcontract or panel work. Fit: **Medium**.
