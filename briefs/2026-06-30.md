# ScaffoldX AU Brief — 30 June 2026

## 1. Culture & General
- **New financial year lands tomorrow (1 July):** the minimum wage rises ~6% to $26.44/hr, award wages rise 4.75%, and the lowest marginal tax rate drops from 16% to 15% — a routine conversation opener with any AU client this week.
- **Vanuatu pact:** Australia signed the "Nakamal Agreement" with Vanuatu, deepening security and economic ties in the Pacific; China has publicly objected, calling it "geopolitical games." Good for small talk on Australia's regional posture.
- **Cricket:** Alyssa Healy is retiring from all formats after 16 years, departing as a dual World Test Championship and ODI World Cup winner — a genuine sporting moment worth a passing mention with clients.

## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **Payday Super goes live 1 July 2026:** employers must now pay super within 7 business days of each payday, calculated on a new "qualifying earnings" definition, and report it via STP. The ATO's Small Business Superannuation Clearing House is closing. This forces a payroll/reporting system review at literally every Australian employer — squarely ScaffoldX territory.
- **Bank Australia and P&N Group (P&N Bank, BCU Bank)** signed an MOU to explore a ~$30bn merger to create Australia's largest customer-owned bank (530,000 customers, 1,500 staff). Leadership explicitly cited the need for "technological muscle" — due diligence and tech-transformation planning now underway.
- **Magellan Financial Group and Barrenjoey** are merging in a $1.6bn deal, consolidating two financial-services platforms — typical trigger for reporting and back-office system rationalisation.
- **AI stuck in pilot purgatory:** 61% of Australian firms report efficiency gains from AI, but only 28% have scaled 40%+ of pilots into production — a clear gap between experimentation and operational AI/agentic deployment.
- **Qantas** opened a new technology and AI-focused innovation centre, expected to create hundreds of roles — signals continued enterprise investment in AI/automation capability building.
