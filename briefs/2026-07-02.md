## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **Payday Super started today (1 July 2026).** Employers must now remit SG contributions within seven business days of every payday, not quarterly. The ATO's Small Business Superannuation Clearing House is being retired, and the SG Charge now applies per pay-cycle. This is a live payroll/reporting-system forcing function for every Australian employer.
- **AUSTRAC Tranche 2 AML/CTF reforms are in effect.** ~80,000 new "designated service" businesses — lawyers, accountants, real estate agents, trust and company service providers — must enrol with AUSTRAC by 29 July and stand up AML programs, customer due diligence, sanctions/PEP screening and 7-year record-keeping. One of the largest single-sector compliance expansions since 2006.
- **Canberra signs 5-year Microsoft deal.** A whole-of-government volume-sourcing agreement for AI and cloud (including Copilot) commenced 1 July, signalling a major public-sector automation push that will flow through to contractors and integrators.
- **Qoria shareholders approve Aura Consolidated Group takeover.** The ASX-listed cybersecurity/ed-tech firm heads toward US ownership via a scheme of arrangement, ahead of an ASX suspension — a fresh systems and reporting integration underway.
- **Qantas' Adelaide Product Innovation Centre goes live.** First staff started this quarter on a build toward ~400 tech roles (software, data, AI) — another data point on enterprise-scale AI/digital investment continuing through mid-2026.
