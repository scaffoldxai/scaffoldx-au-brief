## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **Payday Super started 1 July 2026** — the biggest superannuation compliance change in a decade. Employers must now remit super within 7 business days of every payday (not quarterly), with automatic SGC penalties for misses. The ATO's Small Business Super Clearing House has closed permanently, forcing every employer to have a working payroll-to-fund pipeline. Massive forced process/reporting overhaul across all mid-market employers.
- **ASIC/APRA are streamlining the Financial Accountability Regime (FAR)** — cutting accountability-map update requirements roughly in half for ~2,000 AFSL/ADI licensees and 4,500 accountable persons, effective by end-2026. Firms will need to rebuild their accountability-mapping and reporting workflows to match.
- **Gold sector M&A gold rush**: Genesis Minerals tabled a $5.6bn (~$12.6bn combined) proposal for Vault Minerals, topping Regis Resources' rival bid, to create WA's dominant Leonora-Laverton gold producer with ~$2bn in flagged synergies. Regis retains a matching right until 10 July.
- **Federal government's 5-year Microsoft deal** kicked in 1 July, giving the APS volume access to Copilot, M365, Azure and Dynamics 365 — a strong national signal that Microsoft-stack AI adoption is now default policy, likely flowing down to state agencies and contractors.
- **Compliance-driven automation pressure**: Australian businesses will spend over $78bn on compliance in 2026, with ~60% still running on spreadsheets, email and manual document trails; 2026 SOCI Act reforms (mandatory incident reporting, Enhanced Cyber Security Obligations) are pushing critical-infrastructure and mid-market firms toward continuous, automated evidence trails instead of manual spreadsheets.
