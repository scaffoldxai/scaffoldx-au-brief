## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **RBA held the cash rate at 4.35%** this month after three straight rises, citing slowing growth and an oil-supply shock — capex and tech-spend decisions among mid-market clients likely stay cautious near-term.
- **APRA and ASIC announced Financial Accountability Regime (FAR) reforms** (16 June, implementation through end-2026): higher materiality thresholds for notifying regulators, fewer accountability-map updates, streamlined AFSL competency evidence from October. Less red tape, but entities still need to rebuild how they track and report accountability.
- **72% of Australian CFOs plan to increase ERP investment in 2026** (IDC), with cloud-first, AI-augmented ERP cited as the dominant trend — a live budget conversation for finance leaders right now.
- **Federal government's five-year Microsoft deal** kicked off 1 July, giving the APS access to Copilot, M365, Azure and Dynamics 365 — a major public-sector modernisation push that will ripple into state agencies and their vendor panels.
- **Australian M&A is picking up** — Forbes flagged a "new gold rush" of dealmaking (7 July), and the Genesis–Vault gold merger (creating Australia's #3 gold producer) plus Forrestania's $300m Edna May acquisition show consolidation accelerating across sectors, not just resources.
