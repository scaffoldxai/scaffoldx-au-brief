## 2. Business & Market Signals
- **Payday Super** is now mandatory (from 1 July): employers must remit super in step with every pay run, not quarterly, and the ATO's Small Business Superannuation Clearing House has been retired — a direct payroll-to-super integration problem for any business still on manual or batch processes.
- **AUSTRAC's AML/CTF Tranche 2** takes effect 1 July, pulling real estate professionals, precious metals/stones dealers and professional services firms into reporting obligations for the first time, alongside new TTR/SMR forms for existing reporting entities — a compliance-and-reporting build-out for thousands of newly captured firms.
- **Bank tech leadership reshuffle**: CBA has elevated Victoria Ledda to permanent Group CIO, while Westpac has poached ex-Macquarie CIO Richard Heeley to "accelerate the technology agenda" and drive an Azure-powered infrastructure overhaul — fresh mandates typically trigger new vendor reviews within the first 100 days.
- **Enterprise agentic AI is scaling fast**: Wesfarmers has signed a multi-year deal with Google Cloud to deploy agentic AI across Kmart, Officeworks, Priceline and OnePass, and Bunnings' generative-AI assistant "Buddy" is now live — large AU retailers are normalising agent-based automation, which lowers the sell-in bar for mid-market equivalents.
- **M&A at a four-year high**: AU deal volume hit USD52bn across 451 deals in 1H26 (+23% YoY), headlined by Steadfast's AUD7.7bn takeover approach from Amwins/Dragoneer and the Genesis Minerals–Vault Minerals gold merger (~AUD8.5bn combined) — every completed deal creates a systems-consolidation and PMO-reporting problem.
