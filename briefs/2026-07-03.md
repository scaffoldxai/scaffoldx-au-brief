- **AI adoption past the pilot stage**: new enterprise data shows 80% of Australian organisations have AI assistants deployed beyond pilot and 72% are advancing autonomous agents — but 53% rate their AI security posture as "catching up" or reactive, a governance gap ScaffoldX can speak to.

- **FAR reporting simplification flagged by ASIC/APRA**: regulators are consulting on cutting accountability-map and reporting burden for ~4,500 accountable persons under the Financial Accountability Regime, aiming to implement by end-2026 — financial services clients will need to rebuild compliance reporting workflows once rules land, not just cut them.
