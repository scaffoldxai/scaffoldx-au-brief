## 2. BUSINESS & MARKET SIGNALS

- **AML/CTF Tranche 2 goes live 1 July.** Roughly 90,000 previously unregulated businesses — lawyers, accountants, conveyancers, real estate agents, and dealers in precious metals — must comply with anti-money laundering obligations for the first time. AUSTRAC enrolment closes 29 July; penalties of up to $18,780/day apply for corporations after that. Most of these firms have zero existing compliance infrastructure.

- **Mandatory climate reporting expands to Group 2.** Mid-sized entities with ≥$200M revenue, ≥$500M in assets, or ≥250 employees must begin reporting Scope 1 and 2 emissions plus climate governance under Australian Sustainability Reporting Standards from 1 July. A new data-collection-to-reporting pipeline is non-negotiable from this financial year.

- **Microsoft targets construction as Australia's next AI frontier.** Microsoft's ANZ VP declared that the biggest untapped AI productivity gains lie in field-based industries — construction, mining, and energy. Lendlease and John Holland are already running AI-powered safety monitoring and schedule optimisation at scale. The market expectation is moving faster than most tier-2 builders can respond.

- **Only 12% of Australian organisations say AI is genuinely transforming them.** A June 2026 report (IT Brief / Scale Suite) finds most businesses use generative AI for task shortcuts — email drafting, summarisation — without redesigning underlying workflows. The gap between AI tool adoption and real process transformation is the defining business theme of 2026.

- **Australian startup funding up 42% year-to-date.** $1.77B raised across 82 equity rounds and 76 acquisitions completed so far in 2026. Tech, healthcare, and energy lead. Post-raise and post-acquisition systems fragmentation is a consistent and growing integration pain point.
