## 2. BUSINESS & MARKET SIGNALS

- **Regulatory cliff on 1 July — five obligations, five agencies, one day.** AML/CTF Tranche 2 expands to lawyers, accountants, real estate agents and conveyancers. Mandatory climate-related financial disclosures commence. ASIC's chair called it "the biggest changes to financial reporting in a generation." Most affected businesses remain unprepared.

- **Healthtech Everlab closes AU$65M Series A.** The preventative health platform integrates diagnostics, clinicians, specialists, prescriptions and wearables into a single system. Backed by AirTree and Left Lane, the company raised $15M seed less than a year ago and is now targeting UK expansion. Rapid operational scaling imminent.

- **AI adoption climbing but impact remains shallow.** Deloitte's 2026 State of AI report finds 60%+ of large Australian enterprises use AI, yet only 30% are fundamentally transforming workflows (vs. 34% globally). Most are automating existing processes rather than redesigning them — the gap between tooling and transformation is widening.

- **BA, PMO and Power BI hiring at a June high.** Glassdoor shows 1,705 active business analyst roles across Australia this month, up from 560 in March. Power BI and PMO contracts active across NSW Government, ASX-listed firms and mid-market. Companies are buying capability fast or scrambling to fill skills gaps.

- **Aussie M&A shifting to "orchestration" plays.** PwC's 2026 outlook notes tech deals increasingly stitch cloud, data and AI into integrated platforms. $1.77B raised in 82 funding rounds year-to-date; 76 acquisitions completed. Acquired businesses routinely surface integration debt and reporting fragmentation immediately post-close.
