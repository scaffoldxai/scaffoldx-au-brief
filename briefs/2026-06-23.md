## 2. BUSINESS & MARKET SIGNALS

- **AML/CTF enrolment opens for professional services:** AUSTRAC opened enrolment for lawyers, accountants, real estate agents, and precious metals dealers from 31 March, with obligations kicking in 1 July. Up to 100,000 new entities must now build compliance programs, risk assessments, and AUSTRAC reporting — forcing major process and documentation overhauls across entire professional services sectors.

- **Five major regulatory regimes go live 1 July:** Mandatory climate disclosure (TCFD-aligned), new environmental obligations, and three other cross-sector frameworks activate simultaneously next week. Large entities that have delayed their reporting infrastructure build are now in crunch mode.

- **AI moves from pilot to production:** Deloitte confirms 68% of Australian businesses have shifted AI from pilot to production. Only 30% are using it to fundamentally transform operations — the majority are automating existing processes rather than redesigning them. The productivity ceiling for shallow automation is appearing.

- **Asana acquires no-code agent-builder StackAI:** Closed late May. Asana customers are now asking what their workflow stack looks like in an agentic world — and whether to extend or exit. Mid-market clients are particularly unsettled by the consolidation.

- **Mandatory merger notification regime creating tech M&A friction:** Australia's new compulsory ACCC notification regime (live 1 January 2026) is slowing technology acquisitions. Tech named as a primary watch sector; due diligence and integration timelines are stretching, increasing demand for interim process and reporting support.
