## 2. BUSINESS & MARKET SIGNALS

**Privacy Act automated decision-making deadline — six months out** — From 10 December 2026, APP entities must disclose in their privacy policies how personal information is used in substantially automated decisions affecting individuals. OAIC now holds civil penalty powers for non-compliance. Any mid-market firm using AI scoring, algorithmic approvals, or automated HR and credit workflows is on the clock.

**Everlab closes AU$65m Series A** — Melbourne preventative healthtech Everlab raised AU$65m this week, led by Airtree Ventures with Left Lane Capital and Plural. The company has processed 21 million+ biomarker results, serves 20,000 patients, and counts BHP, BCG, and Bain as corporate partners. Funding goes toward clinical and tech infrastructure and a UK expansion starting now.

**69% of Australian enterprises running autonomous AI agents** — Deloitte's 2026 State of AI in the Enterprise report finds nearly seven in ten Australian organisations have deployed autonomous AI agents — shifting from pilots to production. The dominant pattern is integration into existing platforms (Microsoft 365, ServiceNow, Salesforce) rather than standalone builds.

**AEC sector leads globally on digital, but data governance is the new battleground** — itwire reports Australia's architecture, engineering, and construction industry is among the world's most digitally mature, with BIM, digital twins, and AI-enhanced project planning now standard. However, 64.4% of Australian AEC firms are "very" or "extremely" concerned about data ownership when selecting technology vendors — well above the 37.8% global average.

**Microsoft Power Platform June 2026 update** — Released 11 June, the update brings modern adoption dashboards for Power Apps, Power Automate, and Copilot Studio into public preview. Organisations can now track platform usage trends and resource-level analytics across their automation footprint — useful lever for governance conversations with enterprise clients.
